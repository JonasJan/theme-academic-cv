## About Me

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Waterloo under the supervision of [Douglas Stebila](https://www.douglas.stebila.ca). My research focuses on provable security with a special interest in post-quantum constructions and real-world applications. In particular, I worked on the design and analysis of signature schemes and authenticated key encapsualtion mechanisms.

Previously, I received my PhD from Ruhr University Bochum being supervised by [Eike Kiltz](https://informatik.rub.de/kiltz/) and [Peter Schwabe](https://cryptojedi.org/peter/index.shtml).
In the fall of 2024, I visited the University of Washington to work with [Stefano Tessaro](https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~tessaro/).
